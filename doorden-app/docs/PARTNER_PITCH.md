# Doorden Partner Pitch

## The one-liner

**Doorden is the app that pays people to knock on doors, on their own schedule. Partner with us and your brand is behind every bonus paid and every offer a homeowner confirms.**

## The problem we solve

- **Local businesses** (power washing, windows, gutters, lawn care) need steady jobs but can't afford a sales team or pricey ads.
- **People who want flexible income** are stuck with gig apps that burn their car, their gas, and their time.
- **Homeowners** want trusted local pros at a fair, pre-set price.

## How Doorden works

1. A rep picks a neighborhood and knocks doors. No car, no gas, no cost.
2. A homeowner says yes. The rep logs the job in the app at the business's own price.
3. The homeowner **confirms the offer in the app**.
4. The local business accepts the free job and does the work.
5. **Doorden pays the rep a 12% bonus, powered by you.**

## Why partner with us

| You get | Why it matters |
|---------|----------------|
| **Your brand on every bonus** | "Rep bonuses powered by [You]" shows on every payout and every offer a homeowner confirms |
| **Verified homeowners, not clicks** | Every lead is a real homeowner who confirmed a real job in the app |
| **Hyper-local reach** | Pick the neighborhoods and cities you want to show up in |
| **A good story** | You're funding local jobs and flexible income, not just buying ads |
| **Early-partner pricing** | First partners lock in rates and category exclusivity |

## Who we're talking to

Brands that want to reach homeowners: home insurance, home security, solar, mortgage and refinancing, local banks and credit unions, real estate brokerages, home improvement retailers, and internet/utility providers.

## Partnership options (draft, set your own numbers)

| Option | How it works |
|--------|--------------|
| **Per verified job** | You pay a set fee for every job a homeowner confirms. Simple, pay-for-results. |
| **Neighborhood sponsor** | Monthly flat fee to be the bonus partner in chosen neighborhoods or a whole city. |
| **Category exclusive** | Be the only insurance (or solar, or bank) partner on Doorden. Premium rate. |

Set every option so it covers the 12% rep bonus **plus** Doorden's margin.

## What to bring to the meeting

- [ ] A live demo of the app (run it on your phone with Expo Go, see the README)
- [ ] Your launch city and first neighborhoods
- [ ] Early numbers once you have them: reps signed up, doors knocked, offers confirmed, jobs accepted
- [ ] Signed local businesses (even 3 to 5 is a strong start)
- [ ] A one-page agreement draft reviewed by your lawyer

## Notes before you pitch

- **Don't promise numbers you don't have yet.** Lead with the model and your pilot plan, then add real stats as they come in.
- **Homeowner data needs consent.** If partners will contact homeowners directly, add a separate opt-in checkbox for that on the verify screen and cover it in your Privacy Policy.
